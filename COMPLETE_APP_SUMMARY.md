The quiz generates a complete 6-step routine:

1. **🧼 Cleanser** - Gentle yet effective cleaning
2. **💧 Toner** - Balancing & prepping skin
3. **✨ Serum/Essence** - Targeted treatment
4. **🌱 Moisturizer** - Hydration & protection
5. **☀️ Sunscreen** - Daily UV protection
6. **⚗️ Treatment/Mask** - Weekly boost

**Each step includes:**
- 3 product recommendations
- Brand names & prices (in INR)
- Specific benefits explained
- Why this step matters for YOU
